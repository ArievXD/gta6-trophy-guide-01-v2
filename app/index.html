Roadmap to the Platinum

Five stages, in order. Story first, then strangers, then collectibles, then the activity grinds, then money and 100%. About 200 hours in total; there are no difficulty trophies and no online trophies, so it is long, not hard.

Save rules — read this before you start

  • There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.
  • The only missable trophy is Positive Mental Attitude: meditate on Lucia’s balcony in Chapter 1, or that spot is gone.
  • Several trophies need Lucia and Jason together — most of the list opens up once both are playable in Chapter 2.
  • Robbery trophies open in Chapters 3 and 4. Cheats switch trophies (and saving) off for the session, so play clean.
